    Calculation rules & scope

    Stock and value

    Opening stock + received purchases − dispatched sales − sample issues = closing stock. Each SKU has a fixed unit cost; inventory value is closing units × that cost. Purchase receipt value is the cost of goods received, not cash paid to suppliers. VAT, returns, transfers, reservations and supplier payments are outside this demonstration.

    Current stock health

    Low stock means on-hand units are at or below the SKU reorder point. Otherwise, more than 90 days of cover, or positive stock with no period sales, is labelled Excess. Cover = closing stock ÷ average daily sales during the selected period. The stock ledger uses this historical view; the planning table evaluates the future balance separately.

    30-day baseline and replenishment

    For each product–warehouse pair, forecast demand = sales during the last 30 observed calendar days ÷ observed days × 30, rounded up to whole units before aggregation. Zero-sale days are included. Only history available by the snapshot is used. This simple baseline has no seasonality adjustment or measured accuracy claim.

    Projected net stock = on hand + qualifying purchase quantities expected in the next 30 days − baseline demand. Only orders placed and still unreceived at the snapshot qualify. Unreceived orders due on or before the snapshot are excluded until their delivery dates are confirmed; open orders do not necessarily mean goods are in transit.

    Shortage means projected units < 0; Reorder means projected units ≥ 0 but below the reorder point. Suggested order = max(0, reorder point − projected units). Otherwise, cover above 90 days or positive stock without recent sales is Excess; remaining positions are Healthy. Planning cover uses trailing daily demand. Forecast samples, returns, reservations, lead times and minimum order quantities are not modelled.

    Receivables and timing

    Outstanding = invoices issued through period end − payments received through that date. An unpaid invoice is overdue when its due date is before period end. Collections measure receipts during the selected period, including payments for earlier invoices. Sales and collections therefore need not match. Each demonstration invoice contains one SKU so product filters remain consistent.

    Fixed demonstration

    July–September 2026, with opening stock on 30 June. All dates use calendar days. Period filters change flows and the closing snapshot; warehouse and category filters apply throughout. This browser-based portfolio example does not connect to an employer system or place orders.
