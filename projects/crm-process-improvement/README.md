# CRM Process Improvement

[Read the case study](https://wendyj92.github.io/projects/crm-process-improvement/) · [Portfolio](https://wendyj92.github.io/)

An English process-design case covering the relationship between orders, stock and payments.

- **As-Is:** a reconstructed current-state map based on reported operating issues. It is not a verified legacy SOP and needs stakeholder validation.
- **To-Be:** proposed v0.2 with field validation, order checks, standard-payment and sub-distributor credit branches, cash receipt, Finance checks, exception approval and a close gate. CRM development and adoption are not represented as completed.

The supplied diagrams and editable files are preserved. Both editable files also contain a third supporting diagram covering dispatch, stock transfers and exception resolution. Editable layouts may differ from the PNGs. Page text adds accessible walkthroughs, a gap-to-control summary and proposed validation work. No customer transactions or measured outcomes are included.

SD means sub-distributor. Standard customers require payment confirmation. An approved SD business step may close under valid credit terms, while the receivable remains monitored. The exception return path, detailed stock handovers, permissions and pilot measures still require agreement. The diagrams are process-design material, not a production specification or deployed integration.
