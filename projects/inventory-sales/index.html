Inventory & Sales Forecasting Dashboard

Follow stock movements, estimate the next 30 days of demand and review products that may need replenishing.

Purchase, sales & inventory · HTML / JavaScript

Open interactive dashboard ↗

Overview

I used the structure of an Excel stock tracker as the starting point for this portfolio dashboard. It brings sales, incoming orders and stock balances into one view using newly invented transactions.

Inside the report

  • Sales and stock trends, product mix and product-level performance.
  • A 30-day inventory outlook and replenishment priorities.
  • Stock movements, purchase orders, receipts and receivables, with CSV downloads.

How the estimate works

A simple baseline extends recent daily sales over the next 30 days. Projected stock equals on-hand stock plus eligible expected receipts, minus baseline demand.

The result is an end-of-period estimate. It does not model daily stockouts, seasonality, supplier lead times or minimum order quantities. The dashboard includes the calculation rules.
