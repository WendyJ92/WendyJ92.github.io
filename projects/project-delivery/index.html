Overview

This demonstration brings three workstreams into a single delivery view. It is designed to make schedule differences and follow-up actions easier to review.

Inside the report

  • Project and status filters for the task table and schedule chart.
  • Planned dates, forecast dates and delays measured in calendar days.
  • Reasons for delay and next steps alongside each task.
  • A CSV download of the selected tasks.

Reading the figures

The report uses a fixed snapshot of 30 September 2026. Metrics follow the selected project; the status filter controls the chart, task table and CSV export.

The tasks and dates are invented for this portfolio. They are not a record of an employer project.
