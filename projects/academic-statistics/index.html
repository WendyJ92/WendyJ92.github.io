Bayesian Inference & Classification

Modelling variation in reaction times and comparing classification methods in R.

University of Exeter · MTHM017: Advanced Topics in Statistics

Read the full case study ↗

Overview

This project is part of my MSc Applied Data Science & Statistics coursework at the University of Exeter. It is presented separately from my professional work and independent dashboard demonstrations.

What the project covers

  • Bayesian modelling of reaction-time variation using R and JAGS.
  • Classification methods and evaluation using R and caret.
  • Reported results, methodological limitations and ideas for further development.

Reading the results

The case study summarises the submitted coursework. Classification results come from one 750/250 train/test split; they are not a new benchmark or a rerun of the analysis.

The full write-up explains the methods and reports the figures from the coursework output. The original report and datasets are not bundled with the site.
